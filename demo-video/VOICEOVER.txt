WARRANT — VOICEOVER, FOR RECORDING
==================================

READ THIS FIRST (30 seconds)

  Your own voice. The judge said, unprompted: "Don't use AI voices. It feels
  less genuine to me." She scores presentation.

  Flat delivery. Do not sell. The material is strong enough; performing it
  makes it sound like it isn't. When in doubt, read it slower and quieter.

  4:00 is enforced. "Everything that you put after four minutes, we will not
  watch." This script is 492 spoken words — measured, not estimated. That is
  3:23 at 145 wpm, or 3:38 if you take it slow. You have 20-35 seconds of
  headroom for silence and for the picture to breathe. SPEND IT ON BLOCK 8.
  If you still run long, cut BLOCK 11 first, then BLOCK 6. Never cut 4, 5,
  9 or 12.

  Record each block as its own take. Do not try to do it in one pass. Leave
  two seconds of silence at the head and tail of every take so the edit has
  handles.

  Pause where you see  /  . One beat. They are load-bearing.

  ── MANDATORY ── Google Cloud must be visible on screen somewhere in the cut.
  Console, Cloud Run dashboard, Vertex logs, or the .run URL. Asked whether a
  live UI plus the Cloud Run dashboard counts as proof, the answer was "Yes."
  BLOCK 9 is where it lives. Do not cut both halves of it.

CAREFUL — the instrument.

  BLOCK 12 says no tool reported a number, which is the gate refusing. That's
  honest and it's the strongest beat in the film. But WARRANT_INSTRUMENT_KEYS
  is empty on the deployed service, so nothing on the live site can reach
  "measured". Show the instrument on the bench if you show it at all. Do not
  imply the hosted URL is taking signed readings today.

NEVER.

  Do not stage a reading. Do not type a number and film it as measured.
  Movie magic for framing and lighting; never for the number. It is the one
  move that could actually disqualify a project whose whole thesis is that
  records should be evidence.
